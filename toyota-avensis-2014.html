Vehicle Description

This 2014 Toyota Avensis 2.0 D-4D Tourer is a practical and comfortable diesel estate offering plenty of interior and luggage space. Finished in white, it features a 6-speed manual gearbox, cruise control, touchscreen multimedia system, dual-zone climate control, alloy wheels and a comfortable part-leather and suede-style interior.

The vehicle has covered approximately 102,700 miles and comes with majority service history, a long MOT, two remote keys and a 3-month warranty. It is well suited to families, motorway driving or anyone requiring a spacious and economical estate vehicle.
